Olfa D'Angelo

Olfa is a researcher in applied physics. She studies how gravity — an invisible yet omnipresent force field — shapes the behaviour of matter, and designs experiments for extreme environments, weightlessness in particular, to observe the hidden mechanisms that structure it.

Her artistic practice questions how we perceive and represent what surrounds us: between visible and invisible, between human sensation and machine perception. Within studio bunt she designs and modifies drawing machines that use or record data, including data from her scientific work and data collected by the machines themselves from their environment. The machines and the algorithms are the tools that translate data into drawings — an interface that gives back a perception of the world different from our own.
